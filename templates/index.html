The selection of the top 20 hospitals in Algeria for future use in training and clustering models using federated learning is driven by the diversity and abundance of available data. We currently use only low-resolution, non-clinical data downloaded from Kaggle and the US National Institutes of Health (NIH), which ensures no bias in our prediction models.

Training in Top 20 Hospitals

High-precision training models in the top 20 hospitals in Algeria to ensure the best results in the future, not now. The available models are only trained on data on Kaggle.

Aggregating models to eliminate bias in diagnosis and ensure fairness in results
